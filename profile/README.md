# Looks Legit

**Looks legit. But is it?**

Looks Legit is building a calmer way to check suspicious websites before people enter card details, share personal information, download files, or forward a link to somebody else.

The product is evidence-led: it separates observed facts, weak signals, missing checks, and unsupported claims. A site being registered, encrypted, old, or polished is useful context, but none of those things prove it is safe.

## Private beta

The invited private beta is focused on website checks first. It is not a public launch, and email investigations are planned later.

To register interest, visit [looksleg.it](https://looksleg.it/) or email [hello@looksleg.it](mailto:hello@looksleg.it?subject=Looks%20Legit%20private%20beta).

## What we care about

- Clear evidence over vibes.
- Visible uncertainty instead of false reassurance.
- Practical next steps for people who need to decide quickly.
- Private reports and reviewed corrections before broader public workflows.

## Current boundaries

Looks Legit does not promise that a website is safe. The private beta is designed to help people pause, inspect the available evidence, and make a better-informed decision.
